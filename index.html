      Questions

      Good questions, honest answers.

      Will Ebi be free?

      You’ll be able to start for free. Optional premium family plans will add more. Founding Families get a preferential rate, and we’ll confirm the terms before the pilot opens.

      Who can see my family’s information?

      Only the relatives you invite. Family Circles let you decide which relatives see what, so caregiving updates can stay with the carers while the big moments go to everyone.

      We already have a family tree on Ancestry or MyHeritage.

      Bring it with you. Ebi imports standard family-tree files (GEDCOM), then adds what those tools leave out: what your family calls each other, who raised whom, and the stories behind the names.

      What if relatives remember things differently?

      Ebi keeps both accounts and asks the right relatives to confirm. One person’s version never silently overwrites another’s.

      When can we start?

      We’re building the first version now. The pilot opens in stages, and Founding Families are invited first, in the order they join.

      Is Ebi only for Nigerian families?

      We’re starting with Nigerian families in the UK, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa families together, so we get the cultural detail right. Wider African communities and families of every background come next.

      Does it work on my phone?

      Yes. Ebi works on phones and computers, so Grandma can use the tablet and cousins abroad can use their phones.
